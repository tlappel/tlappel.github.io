I’m Travis Appel. My professional world is building automation: the controllers, networks, graphics, and operational decisions that make complex buildings understandable.

Outside that work, I explore artificial intelligence, machine learning, creative systems, writing, and the infrastructure that helps technology feel more useful and more human. This site is where those threads can live together without pretending they are unrelated.

It is a work in progress, which is probably the most accurate thing I could say about both the site and its author.
